What if the story of Moses is also the story of your own soul?

For more than sixteen centuries, **Gregory of Nyssa** has been treasured as one of Christianity's greatest spiritual guides. A fourth-century bishop and one of the Cappadocian Fathers, he saw in Moses' journey a living picture of every believer's journey into God.

Egypt becomes our bondage to the false self. The Red Sea becomes our freedom. The wilderness becomes the place of transformation. Sinai becomes the ascent of prayer. Every step reveals that the Christian life is not about arriving, but about continually growing into the infinite love of Christ.

This devotional presents Gregory's timeless vision through seventy-five daily reflections written in warm, contemporary language. Rather than a translation or academic commentary, these meditations invite you to walk beside Moses, encounter Christ more deeply, and discover your own story within his.

Whether you are new to the spiritual journey or have walked with Christ for many years, these pages will encourage you to take one more step up the mountain, where every ending becomes another beginning.

The journey into the infinite love of God has only just begun.
