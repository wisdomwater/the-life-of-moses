## Epilogue

If you have reached these final pages, you may feel that you have come to the end of a journey.

Gregory would gently smile and tell you that you have only just begun.

The life of Moses never truly ends. It continues wherever a soul leaves Egypt behind, passes through the waters of grace, learns to trust in the wilderness, climbs the mountain of prayer, enters the holy darkness where God dwells beyond every image, and discovers that every encounter with Him awakens an even greater longing. Moses' story has become your story because Christ has invited you into the same pilgrimage.

This is one of Gregory's greatest gifts to the Church. He frees us from the burden of thinking that the Christian life is about finally "arriving." We need not fear that one day we will reach the end of God's goodness, exhaust His beauty, or complete our transformation. Such an end could never satisfy the human heart, because we were created for the Infinite. The God who calls us is boundless in love, inexhaustible in wisdom, and endless in beauty. Every step into His presence reveals another horizon waiting to be explored.

Do not become discouraged, then, if you still find mountains to climb. Do not lose heart when old weaknesses remind you that you are still on the way. The Spirit who has faithfully led you this far has never expected perfection in a single moment. He delights in your continual becoming. Even your stumbles, when surrendered to Christ, become places where grace teaches you to walk more deeply in humility and trust.

Perhaps that is the greatest lesson Moses leaves us. The promised land was never merely a destination on a map. The true inheritance is life with God Himself. Christ is both the Way and the Land toward which we journey. To follow Him is already to begin tasting the inheritance that one day will fill all things.

So close this book, but do not close your heart.

Keep praying.

Keep forgiving.

Keep wondering.

Keep climbing.

When the path grows steep, remember that the mountain rises into infinite love. When the darkness surrounds you, remember that it may be the brightness of God's mystery hidden from ordinary sight. When your heart aches with longing, remember that longing is not a sign of absence but of invitation. Love calls you onward because Love has no end.

May you become, like Moses, a friend of God.

May your eyes remain undimmed because they are fixed upon the true Light.

May your heart remain forever young because it is continually renewed by the Spirit.

May every ending become another beginning.

And may you discover, both in this life and in the ages to come, that there is always more of Christ to know, more of His beauty to behold, and more of His love to receive.

Go in peace, and keep walking.

The journey has only begun.
