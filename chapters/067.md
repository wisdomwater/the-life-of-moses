## Day 67 — The Fruit of the Living Rod

> *What God appoints bears fruit that only His life can produce.*

Aaron's rod was nothing more than dead wood placed among other pieces of dead wood. Yet when God breathed His life into it, it budded, blossomed, and bore fruit. The miracle was not merely that a rod lived again, but that life appeared where no human effort could produce it. This is how God works in every soul that yields itself to Him.

The true priesthood is never something we seize. It is always something received. Whether you are called to lead many or simply to love those nearest to you, your calling is not established by ambition but by the quiet fruit of God's life growing within you. Wherever Christ is truly present, life begins to blossom.

See also the fruit that Aaron's rod produced. It was not soft and showy on the outside. Its shell was hard, plain, and unassuming, while its nourishment remained hidden within. So it is with those who have learned to live close to God. Their lives may not appear impressive by the world's standards, yet beneath a humble exterior lies patience, gentleness, self-control, compassion, and a wisdom that quietly nourishes others.

Do not seek a life that merely looks beautiful. Seek one that bears the fruit of Christ. The world often prizes comfort, applause, and outward splendor, but the kingdom treasures hearts made strong through surrender. As you remain rooted in Christ, His life will mature within you, and in time others will taste the sweetness that only His Spirit can produce.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Bring Your life through me.*

> **Exhale:** *Bear Your fruit in me.*
