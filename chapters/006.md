## Day 6 — Into the Holy Darkness

> *The nearer you come to God, the more you discover that love is greater than understanding.*

When Israel came to Sinai, they did not first receive commandments—they first encountered the holiness of God. The mountain shook, fire blazed, clouds gathered, and the sound of heaven overwhelmed every earthly voice. Before they could hear God's word, they had to learn that He is never small enough to be mastered by the human mind. Reverence is the beginning of wisdom because it teaches us that God is infinitely greater than our thoughts of Him.

Yet notice where Moses' journey leads. The people remained at the foot of the mountain, but Moses continued upward. He passed through the fire, beyond the cloud, and at last entered the darkness where God was. This may seem strange, for we often imagine that drawing near to God means moving into ever greater clarity. But I tell you that there is a light so brilliant that, to our limited understanding, it appears as darkness. The mystery of God is not the absence of His presence but the abundance of it.

Do not fear those seasons when your prayers seem quieter than before, or when God no longer fits neatly within your explanations. If you remain faithful, you may simply be leaving behind childish certainties so that you can enter a deeper communion. The soul that loves God does not possess Him as an object to be examined. It rests in Him as a child rests in the embrace of a loving father whose greatness can never be exhausted.

This is why we must be careful not to fashion God in the image of our own understanding. Whatever you can completely comprehend is smaller than the One who created heaven and earth. Let your thoughts lead you toward Him, but do not mistake your thoughts for Him. Christ has truly revealed the Father, yet even in Christ we are invited into an inexhaustible mystery of divine love that stretches beyond the horizon of all human knowledge.

Therefore, do not cease your ascent when certainty comes to an end. Continue climbing. Beyond every answer lies a greater wonder, and beyond every glimpse of God's goodness lies another invitation to know Him more deeply. This is the perfection for which you were made—not that you would finally arrive, but that you would rejoice forever in the endless discovery of the One who is Love.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Lead me beyond sight.*

> **Exhale:** *Into Your mystery.*
