## Day 31 — Rest Along the Way

> *God refreshes us, not so that we may stop the journey, but so that we may continue it.*

The wilderness is not an endless stretch of hardship. After the bitter waters came cool springs and the welcome shade of tall palms. God knows that those who climb the mountain cannot live by struggle alone. Along the way, He prepares places where weary souls may drink deeply and recover their strength.

These resting places are His gifts. Sometimes they come through the wisdom of Scripture. Sometimes through the encouragement of faithful companions. Sometimes through quiet prayer, the beauty of creation, or the simple peace that settles over the heart when we remember that Christ is with us. Every true refreshment is a spring flowing from Him.

Yet even these blessings are not the destination. Israel camped beneath the palms, but they did not build a city there. The soul must learn both to receive God's consolations with gratitude and to release them when the Cloud begins to move again. Every resting place is meant to strengthen your desire for the greater country that still lies ahead.

As you continue your ascent, remember that Christ Himself is the Rock from whom living water flows. To the closed heart He may seem hard and unyielding, but to the one who approaches in faith He becomes an inexhaustible fountain. Drink deeply of Him, and you will discover that His presence can refresh you even in the driest wilderness.

The journey continues, but you do not walk it alone. The One who calls you onward also provides every spring your soul will need until, at last, you drink from the river of life itself.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *You are my living water.*

> **Exhale:** *Refresh me as I walk.*
