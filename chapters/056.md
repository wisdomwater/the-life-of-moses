## Day 56 — Always Another Step

> *The soul never outgrows the next invitation of love.*

Consider the path Moses walked. He left the palace behind. He chose the wilderness over comfort. He stood before the burning bush. He crossed the sea, ascended the mountain, entered the cloud, received the Law, destroyed the idols, and beheld the glory of God. Yet after all these wonders, he did not settle into contentment. He continued to climb.

This is the mystery of true growth. We often imagine perfection as arriving at a place where nothing more remains to learn. But the life of God is not a destination that can be exhausted. Because God is infinite, every summit reveals another horizon. Each act of faith enlarges your heart for a greater love than it could have imagined before.

Do not measure your progress by how far you have traveled compared to others. Measure it by whether your heart still says yes to the next step. Even small acts of forgiveness, quiet prayer, hidden kindness, and patient trust become rungs on the ladder that reaches into the life of God. The ascent is made one faithful step at a time.

Take courage if the mountain still rises before you. That is not because you have failed to climb. It is because the God who calls you upward is endlessly beautiful. His invitation will never end, and neither will the joy of answering it.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** Lead me one step higher.

> **Exhale:** I will keep following.
