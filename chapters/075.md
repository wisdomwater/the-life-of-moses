## Day 75 — Ever Beginning

> *The closer you come to God, the more beautifully the journey begins again.*

You may have expected Moses' story to end with arrival. Instead, it ends with ascent. Even after all the wonders he witnessed, all the mountains he climbed, and all the conversations he shared with God, there was still more of God before him than behind him. This is the holy mystery of the spiritual life: the destination is not a place where desire comes to rest, but an endless communion in which love continually grows.

Do not be troubled if you still feel a longing that nothing in this world can satisfy. That longing is not a sign that something is missing. It is the imprint of the Infinite within you. Every fresh glimpse of God's goodness awakens a deeper capacity to receive Him. Love does not exhaust itself in possession. It is enlarged by participation.

This is why the journey of Moses becomes the journey of every disciple of Christ. We leave Egypt, pass through the waters, eat the bread from heaven, climb the mountain, enter the cloud, behold the cross, and learn the joy of friendship with God. Yet each grace becomes an invitation to another. Christ is not merely the end of the road; He is the living Way upon which we walk forever, leading us from glory to glory without end.

So let this not be the conclusion of your pilgrimage, but another beginning. Walk into tomorrow with the quiet confidence that God is always more beautiful than you have yet imagined, more loving than you have yet experienced, and nearer than you have yet discovered. The infinite life of God stretches before you, and every faithful step reveals another horizon of His goodness.

May you become God's friend by continually saying yes to His invitation. May your heart never cease to hunger for His presence. And may your joy forever be to follow Christ, who always leads His beloved children farther up and farther in.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Lead me ever onward.*

> **Exhale:** *Farther up and farther in.*
