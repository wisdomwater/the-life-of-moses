## Day 60 — Follow the One Who Passes By

> *We behold God most truly by following where He leads.*

Moses asked to see God face to face, but he was taught instead to watch as God passed by. He would see only what came after, the trace of the One already moving ahead. The mystery is not that God refused to be known, but that He revealed the way He is known: not by being captured in thought, but by being followed.

Christ gives the same invitation. He does not ask you to stand before Him as though you could examine and contain Him. He says, “Follow me.” Walk behind Him into mercy, humility, forgiveness, and self-giving love. Keep His path before your eyes, and you will come to know the One who leads you.

This means that obedience is itself a form of vision. You may not always understand where Christ is taking you, but you can recognize the direction of His footsteps. They lead toward the wounded, the forgotten, the enemy, and the cross. They lead through death into life.

Do not wait until every mystery is resolved before you begin to follow. The road becomes visible beneath His feet. Stay close to Him, and even the darkness will become a place of guidance. You will behold God not by standing still and demanding an explanation, but by walking faithfully in the way of Christ.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Christ, go before me.*

> Exhale: *I will follow.*
