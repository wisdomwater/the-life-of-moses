## Day 16 — The Rod of Faith

> *Christ entered our weakness so that our weakness might share His life.*

The signs God placed into Moses' hands were more than miracles; they whispered the mystery of Christ long before His coming. The strong hand stretched forth in humility. The rod became a serpent and then a rod again. In these wonders we glimpse the astonishing love of God, who did not remain distant from our suffering but entered it completely.

The Son of God took upon Himself everything that belonged to our wounded condition except sin itself. He stepped into the very place where death and evil believed they ruled, not to be overcome by them but to overcome them from within. Like the serpent lifted in the wilderness, Christ entered the place of humanity's sickness so that healing might spring forth where despair once reigned.

Because He descended, you need not fear your own weakness. Bring your failures, your questions, and even the parts of yourself that seem most tangled by sin into His hands. What He takes up is never left unchanged. The same Lord who transformed the serpent into a shepherd's staff is able to transform what once wounded you into something that strengthens your journey.

As you continue climbing the mountain of God, you need not lean upon your own resolve. Let the rod of faith support you. Hope does not rest upon your strength to hold onto Christ, but upon His unwavering strength to hold onto you until love has completed its work.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Christ, uphold me.*

> **Exhale:** *Transform my weakness.*
