## Day 23 — Choosing the Light

> *The light shines on everyone, but each soul decides whether to step into it.*

The darkness that covered Egypt was not the absence of the sun. The light still shone, yet some could not receive its gift. So it is with every soul. God's presence is not reserved for a chosen few while hidden from the rest. His love shines without partiality, but each person decides whether to live with eyes open or closed.

Never imagine that darkness is something God delights to send. We create our own shadows whenever we turn away from the light that continually seeks us. Sin carries its own consequences, just as sickness follows habits that wound the body. God's judgments often reveal what our choices have already begun to shape within us. The Physician uncovers the illness so that healing may finally begin.

Even the darkest night is not beyond hope. If Christ stretched out His hands for those who nailed Him to the cross, can His mercy not also reach into every place where darkness has settled? Never measure the limits of divine compassion by the limits of your own imagination. Continue praying, continue hoping, and continue walking toward the light.

Each day presents the same quiet invitation. You need not conquer the entire mountain today. Simply turn your face again toward the Sun, and let His light do what light has always done—drive away the darkness one dawn at a time.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Turn me toward Your light.*

> **Exhale:** *Lead me ever onward.*
