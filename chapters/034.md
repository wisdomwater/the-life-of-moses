## Day 34 — Preparing for the Eternal Sabbath

> *Every act of love becomes food that does not perish.*

The manna kept for ordinary days spoiled by morning, but what was gathered for the Sabbath remained fresh. Do not think this is merely a lesson about ancient customs. It is a window into your own life. Every day you are preparing for a greater rest than sleep—a Sabbath where your labor gives way to joyful communion with God.

This present life is your preparation. Here you sow the seeds that eternity will bring to fullness. Every act of mercy, every hidden prayer, every forgiveness offered, every quiet surrender to Christ is gathered into God's keeping. Nothing shaped by love is ever wasted. What seems small today becomes incorruptible in His kingdom.

Likewise, whatever is planted in selfishness cannot endure. The false self spends its strength gathering what time cannot preserve, but the Spirit teaches you to invest in what death itself cannot touch. You are becoming even now the kind of person who will dwell forever in God's presence. Eternity is not merely waiting for you ahead; it is quietly growing within you.

So do not become weary in preparing your heart. Every faithful step, however unnoticed, is bread stored for the everlasting Sabbath. And when your earthly journey is complete, you will discover that Christ has been preparing not only a place for you, but also preparing you for that place.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Prepare my heart*

> **Exhale:** *For Your eternal rest*
