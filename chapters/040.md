## Day 40 — Into the Luminous Darkness

> *The closer you draw to God, the more wonderful His mystery becomes.*

At the beginning of the journey, God often meets you in light. There are moments when truth seems wonderfully clear, when Scripture opens before you, when Christ's presence fills your heart with joy. Give thanks for these gifts. They are real, and they strengthen your first steps. Yet do not imagine that the light is the end of the journey.

There comes a time when the path leads into darkness—not the darkness of sin or despair, but the holy mystery where every certainty gives way to deeper trust. Here you discover that God is greater than every thought you have ever had about Him. The names by which you have known Him remain true, yet they can never contain Him. His love is deeper than your understanding, His beauty greater than your imagination, and His life beyond every image your mind can form.

Do not be afraid when prayer becomes quieter and words seem insufficient. This is not because God has withdrawn, but because He is drawing you beyond ideas into communion. The soul that longs for Him eventually discovers that the greatest knowledge is not possessing explanations but standing in loving wonder before the Infinite. To see God is, in one sense, to realize that He can never be fully seen.

This holy unknowing is not emptiness. It is fullness beyond measure. Every step into the mystery awakens greater love, greater humility, and greater desire. You do not lose God in the darkness. You lose only the illusion that He could ever be contained within your understanding. And that is the beginning of true contemplation.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Beyond my knowing*

> **Exhale:** *I rest in You*
