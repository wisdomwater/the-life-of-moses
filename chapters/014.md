## Day 14 — Take Off Your Sandals

> *The light of God is not reached by striving higher, but by laying aside what cannot enter His presence.*

After many quiet years in the wilderness, Moses saw what no searching could have discovered. A bush burned with fire, yet it was not consumed. God often waits until our restless striving has settled before revealing Himself. The soul that has learned to be still begins to notice a fire that was always there—the steady flame of divine love burning within the ordinary places of life.

The bush itself became a promise. The eternal Word clothed Himself in our humanity without destroying it, just as the fire filled the thornbush without consuming it. In Christ, heaven did not reject the earth but embraced it. God entered our fragile flesh so that our humanity might become radiant with His life.

Yet before Moses could draw nearer, he was told to remove his sandals. There are coverings the soul cannot wear in the presence of God. We come carrying old identities, fears, illusions, and the heavy dust of a world that teaches us to trust what passes away. The journey toward God is not about collecting more knowledge but about gently laying aside whatever keeps our feet from touching holy ground.

Do not be afraid if the Lord asks you to release something you have long relied upon. Every burden set down becomes another step into freedom. The fire still burns before you, inviting you not merely to observe its beauty, but to stand within its warmth until your own life begins to shine with the light of Christ.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Strip away what hinders.*

> **Exhale:** *Lead me into Your light.*
