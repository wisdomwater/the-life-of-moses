## Day 57 — Seeing the Back of God

> *To know God is not to contain Him, but to follow Him forever.*

Moses asked to see God as He truly is. The answer was both yes and no. God hid him in the cleft of the rock, covered him with His hand, and allowed him to see only what had already passed by. This is not because God delights in hiding Himself, but because no finite mind can contain the Infinite. We are always following the One who is forever greater than our understanding.

Do not seek a faith that explains everything. A God small enough to fit inside your thoughts would no longer be God. The mystery of God is not a wall that keeps you away but an ocean that continually invites you deeper. Wonder is not the enemy of faith; it is one of its purest expressions.

Christ is the Rock in whom we are safely hidden. In Him we truly know the Father, yet even now we know only according to the measure our hearts can receive. Every revelation is real, but none is complete. Every vision is true, yet there is always more of His goodness waiting beyond the horizon.

This is why the soul never ceases to seek. The true vision of God is not the end of desire but its eternal fulfillment. Love always discovers greater beauty to love. Heaven itself will not be the conclusion of your journey, but the endless beginning of deeper communion with the infinite life of God.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** Hide me in Christ.

> **Exhale:** Lead me ever deeper.
