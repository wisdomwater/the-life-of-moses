## Day 12 — The Ark That Carries You

> *Wisdom does not remove the river; it teaches you how to remain above its waters.*

Life often feels like the Nile that carried the infant Moses—swift, uncertain, and filled with currents beyond our control. The waves of ambition, anxiety, distraction, and desire threaten to pull the soul beneath their surface. We cannot escape the river simply by wishing it away. The question is not whether we will enter its waters, but what will carry us through them.

Moses was not abandoned to the river. He was entrusted to an ark. So too, the Lord does not ask you to navigate this world alone. He gives you practices that quietly keep your soul afloat: prayer, Scripture, worship, the wisdom of those who have walked before you, and every discipline that trains the heart to recognize His presence. These are not burdens but boards fastened together by love, bearing you safely above the flood.

Even then, do not be ashamed of your tears. Moses wept while he was safe within the ark. Tears are not proof that faith has failed; often they are the water by which faith is preserved. A heart that still longs for God has not been overcome by the river, no matter how turbulent its circumstances may seem.

The strange mercy of God is that the very currents we fear often carry us toward firmer ground. What seems determined to overwhelm us can become the means by which He draws us closer to Himself. Trust the ark He has given you, and let the river become another teacher on the journey toward Christ.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Carry me, Lord.*

> **Exhale:** *Above every wave.*
