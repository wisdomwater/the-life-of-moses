## Day 20 — What Lives in the House

> *The habits you welcome become the companions of your soul.*

The frogs of Egypt crept into every room. They covered the tables, entered the bedrooms, and even filled the hidden storehouses. Evil seldom remains where we first permit it to enter. What begins as a small indulgence quietly spreads until it colors the atmosphere of the whole heart.

The house of your soul is built one affection at a time. Whatever you continually gaze upon shapes your imagination. Whatever you repeatedly feast upon nourishes either life or decay. Whatever you hide away in the secret places of your heart eventually becomes part of the person you are becoming. Christ does not merely seek to change your outward behavior; He longs to make your whole dwelling a place where His peace may abide.

This is why the spiritual life is not sustained only by avoiding what is harmful, but by filling every room with what is beautiful, true, and good. Let your eyes linger on what awakens gratitude. Let your table become a place of generosity and simplicity. Let the hidden chambers of your heart be places where prayer quietly keeps watch. As the love of Christ fills the house, there is less and less room for the noise of lesser desires.

Do not become discouraged if you discover a few frogs still hiding in forgotten corners. The Lord does not expose them to shame you but to free you. He is a patient guest who cleanses every room He enters, until your life itself becomes a dwelling place for the living God.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Cleanse my heart.*

> **Exhale:** *Make me Your dwelling.*
