## Day 50 — Faith That Rings True

> *A faithful life speaks as clearly as faithful words.*

Among the priestly garments were golden bells that rang as the priest walked, alternating with ripe pomegranates full of hidden sweetness. This is a beautiful picture of the Christian life. Faith must have a clear voice, but it must also bear living fruit. Words without transformed character become empty echoes, while virtue without love for God loses its deepest source.

The pomegranate teaches us another quiet lesson. Its rough exterior hides a richness that only patience can discover. So too the life of discipleship often appears demanding at first. Forgiveness costs us. Self-control stretches us. Humility wounds our pride. Yet beneath these difficult beginnings grows a sweetness that cannot be found by any easier path.

Do not judge your journey by today's discomfort. The Gardener knows when every fruit is ready to open. Many of the virtues you struggle to cultivate now will one day become your deepest joy. What feels like discipline today may become delight tomorrow.

So let your life ring with truth like the priest's bells, and let your heart ripen like the hidden fruit. Together they proclaim the beauty of Christ more clearly than either could alone.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Strengthen my faith*

> **Exhale:** *Bear Your fruit in me*
