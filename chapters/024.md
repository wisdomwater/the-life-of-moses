## Day 24 — Crush the First Seed

> *Great evils begin as small permissions.*

Do not wait until sin has become a giant before you seek God's help. The enemy rarely enters your life with a shout. More often he arrives as a whisper—a resentment you choose to nurse, a temptation you decide to entertain, a fear you refuse to surrender. What seems small today can become tomorrow's captivity if it is allowed to take root.

This is why the death of Egypt's firstborn points us beyond the history to the life of the soul. Do not imagine that God delights in destruction. Rather, learn what He is showing you: if the first beginning of evil is put to death, its bitter harvest never comes. Anger left unchecked grows into cruelty. Lust matures into unfaithfulness. Pride blossoms into a life turned inward upon itself. Crush the serpent's head while it is still within reach.

Do not be discouraged when you discover these first stirrings within yourself. They are not proof that you have failed. They are invitations to become attentive. The Holy Spirit gently teaches you to notice the first movement away from love so that you may return before your feet have wandered far. Every small act of repentance is a quiet victory that prevents countless sorrows yet unseen.

And how shall you overcome these beginnings? Not by your own strength alone, but by the blood of the true Lamb. The doorposts of your heart are marked by Christ before the destroyer ever arrives. As you entrust your mind, your desires, and your strength to Him, every part of your soul begins to find its proper harmony again. What was divided is made whole. What was vulnerable becomes a dwelling fit for God's presence.

So watch over the small beginnings. The soul is shaped not only by great decisions but by a thousand quiet choices made each day. Choose love while it is still small, and you will discover that grace has already begun to overcome what once threatened to master you.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Guard my heart, O Christ.*

> **Exhale:** *Let love grow first.*
