## Day 63 — The Grapes Upon the Wood

> *The promise of the kingdom is carried to us through the cross of Christ.*

Joshua returned from the promised land carrying a cluster of grapes suspended upon wood. What seemed only a sign of a fertile country was already pointing beyond itself. Long before the cross stood upon Calvary, another piece of wood quietly proclaimed the abundance of God's kingdom. Every promise hidden in Moses finds its fullness in Christ.

Look carefully at the fruit. It is not merely evidence that the land exists; it is a foretaste placed into the hands of those still wandering. So too, Christ does not merely tell us that eternal life awaits us. He gives Himself to us even now. His life, poured out like the fruit of the vine, becomes the drink that strengthens weary pilgrims until they reach the fullness of the kingdom.

Whenever the road through the wilderness feels long, remember the grapes upon the wood. The cross is not simply the place where suffering was endured; it is where the abundance of heaven began overflowing into the world. Every act of forgiveness, every quiet prayer, every victory over the false self draws its life from that inexhaustible vine.

Do not think that you walk toward an uncertain future. Christ has already brought back the first fruits of the land He is leading you into. Taste His love today, and let it awaken an even deeper longing for the endless feast still to come. The One who has shown you the fruit will surely bring you home to the vineyard.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Christ, my true Vine,*

> **Exhale:** *Lead me into Your life.*
