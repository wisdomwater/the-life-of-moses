## Day 44 — Hidden Beyond the Veil

> *The nearer we draw to God, the more gladly we embrace holy mystery.*

The tabernacle contained an outer room where priests could minister and an inner sanctuary hidden behind the veil. This was not because God delights in secrecy, but because His infinite life can never be exhausted by the human mind. There is always another room to enter, another horizon to behold, another depth of love waiting beyond what we already understand.

Do not become discouraged when your questions remain unanswered. There comes a time when the soul discovers that knowing about God and knowing God are not the same journey. The deeper your love grows, the more willingly you release the need to master every mystery. Love is content to kneel where certainty cannot stand.

Even the highest angels veil the mysteries of God with their wings. If they, who dwell nearer than we do, approach with reverent wonder, should we expect to possess complete understanding? Humility is not the end of wisdom but its beginning. The soul grows not by grasping more tightly but by opening itself more fully.

So continue climbing with Moses. Each ascent reveals that God is greater than you imagined yesterday. This is not failure but joy, for an infinite God means an endless journey. You will never reach the place where there is nothing more to discover, because divine love forever invites you deeper.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Lead me beyond certainty*

> **Exhale:** *Into the mystery of Your love*
