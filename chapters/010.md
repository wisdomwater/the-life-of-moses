## Day 10 — The Royal Road

> *The surest path to God is the one that neither wanders to the right nor to the left.*

The journey with God is not only about leaving Egypt; it is also about learning how to walk. As Israel drew nearer to the land of promise, they were instructed to remain on the royal road, turning neither to the right nor to the left. This is the way of every soul that desires communion with God. Love does not rush ahead in self-confidence, nor does it drift into distraction. It remains steadfast, fixing its eyes upon Christ and quietly following wherever He leads.

You will discover many voices that promise shortcuts. Some will tempt you with fear, others with pride, and still others with the illusion that power can accomplish what only love can do. Even Balaam, who came intending to curse God's people, found that no darkness can overcome those whose lives are held in the hands of God. The blessing of the Lord cannot be undone by the schemes of men, nor can His purpose be overturned by the powers of evil. Rest, then, in the confidence that your safety lies not in your own strength but in His faithful presence.

Yet there is one danger that often succeeds where outward enemies fail. The heart may withstand persecution while quietly yielding to lesser loves. Israel was not conquered by armies but by disordered desire. So be watchful over your affections. The things that slowly draw your heart away from God are often more dangerous than the trials that openly oppose you. Freedom is preserved not only by resisting evil but by loving what is truly good.

At the end of his earthly pilgrimage, Moses stood upon the mountain and looked into the land he would not enter. Some might call this an unfinished story, but they would misunderstand the journey. Moses' life was never about possessing a place. It was about becoming a person whose whole being had been transformed by the presence of God. Even in old age, his strength had not faded, for the beauty of a soul turned toward God does not wither. The outward person changes, but the one who continually shares in the life of God is renewed from glory to glory.

So do not measure your life by what you have accomplished or accumulated. Measure it by the degree to which love has enlarged your heart. Continue on the royal road with patience, and do not be troubled if the horizon always seems to stretch farther ahead. The infinite goodness of God means that every ending is also another beginning, and every faithful step carries you deeper into the joy for which you were created.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Keep me on Your path.*

> **Exhale:** *Lead me ever deeper.*
