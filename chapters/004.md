## Day 4 — The God Who Makes a Way

> *What seems like an impossible end may become the place where faith first learns to walk.*

When the sea stood before Israel and Pharaoh's army pressed in behind them, there appeared to be no path forward. Fear always tells us that we have reached the end—that there is nowhere left to go and no hope remains. Yet God often begins His greatest works precisely where every human road comes to an end. Moses stepped toward the waters not because he could already see the way, but because he trusted the One who could.

Do not be surprised if your own journey brings you to such places. There are moments when the old life pursues you even after you have chosen freedom. Old fears, familiar temptations, and voices from the past may seem stronger than your desire to follow Christ. Do not turn back toward Egypt. The path to life is often hidden until the moment you take the next faithful step.

When Israel walked through the sea, the waters did not disappear. They remained on either side, held back only by the power of God. Faith is seldom the absence of danger. More often it is the quiet confidence that the Lord is present even while the waters tower around us. What protects us is not our strength but His faithfulness.

Soon after their deliverance, the people grew thirsty and found only bitter water. Yet even there, God provided what was needed, turning bitterness into sweetness through a piece of wood. Learn to recognize the wisdom hidden within this sign. Again and again, Christ takes what is bitter in our lives and, through the wood of His cross, transforms it into a spring that gives life. The trials you fear today may become tomorrow's testimony of His goodness.

Walk on without fear. The God who opens seas also sweetens bitter waters, and He never leads His children into the wilderness without preparing grace for every step.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Lead me through the waters.*

> **Exhale:** *I trust Your way.*
