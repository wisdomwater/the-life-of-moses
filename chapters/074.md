## Day 74 — The Friend of God

> *The highest calling is not success, but friendship with God.*

Moses reached the end of his earthly pilgrimage without ever truly reaching the end of God. Even as he stood upon the mountain, there was still more beauty before him than behind him. Scripture calls him the servant of the Lord, yet God also speaks of him as His friend. These are not two different honors. They are one reality. The one who gives himself completely to God discovers that loving service blossoms into intimate friendship.

Do not imagine that God desires your obedience while keeping you at a distance. Christ has revealed something far greater. He calls His disciples friends because He shares His own life with them. The goal of your journey is not merely to become a better person or a more disciplined believer. It is to live in such communion with God that His desires become your desires, and His love becomes the atmosphere in which your whole life unfolds.

This is why the true spiritual life cannot be driven by fear of punishment or by the hope of reward alone. Such motives belong to the beginning of the journey. Love carries you farther. As your heart awakens to the goodness of God, you begin to seek Him not for what He gives but because He Himself has become your greatest joy. To lose His friendship becomes the only real sorrow; to abide in His presence becomes your deepest delight.

This is perfection—not that you have arrived, but that your whole being has become oriented toward God. Every step forward awakens another desire for Him. Every glimpse of His beauty invites another ascent. Eternity itself will not exhaust this friendship, for the infinite God will forever have more of Himself to give, and the soul will forever have greater joy in receiving Him.

Walk on, then, with Moses. Walk on with Christ. The mountain still rises before you, but so does the endless love of God. And that is the happiest journey of all.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Draw me into Your friendship.*

> **Exhale:** *You are my greatest joy.*
