## Day 65 — Lift Your Eyes

> *The poison of sin loses its power when our eyes remain fixed on Christ.*

Our desires can become like serpents hidden among the stones of the wilderness. They strike when we least expect them, filling the heart with fear, resentment, pride, or restless longing. Though these passions promise life, they quietly drain it away. Left to themselves, they lead us farther from the freedom for which we were created.

Yet God answers our wounds in a surprising way. He does not ask us first to conquer every passion by our own strength. Instead, He tells us to look. Moses lifted up the bronze serpent, and those who turned their eyes toward it lived. In the fullness of time, Christ was lifted up upon the cross so that everyone who looks to Him with faith might find healing deeper than the wound itself.

To look upon the cross is more than remembering an event from long ago. It is to allow the love revealed there to reshape your whole life. As Christ gives Himself without reserve, so you learn to loosen the grip of the false self. Little by little, what once ruled you loses its authority as the life of Christ grows stronger within you.

Do not be discouraged if the old desires still whisper. The serpents are not gone simply because we have begun to believe. Even the faithful feel their bite from time to time. The difference is that we no longer face them alone. Whenever temptation rises, lift your eyes again to the Crucified One. His love is the medicine that weakens the poison until, through countless acts of grace, your heart learns to desire Him above all else.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Jesus, lifted up for me,*

> **Exhale:** *Heal my wandering heart.*
