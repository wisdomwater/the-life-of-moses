## Day 41 — No Image Can Contain Him

> *The living God is always greater than the God we imagine.*

When Moses entered the cloud, he learned something that every soul must one day discover: God cannot be reduced to an image, an idea, or a definition. The mind naturally reaches for comparisons, trying to grasp the Infinite with finite thoughts. Yet whatever can be completely captured by your imagination cannot be the One who created imagination itself.

This is why idols are not only carved from wood or stone. We fashion them whenever we mistake our ideas about God for God Himself. Even our finest theology must remain humble enough to bow before the mystery it seeks to describe. The purpose of truth is not to imprison God within our understanding, but to lead us into living relationship with Him.

Christ reveals the Father more perfectly than any concept ever could. In Him we truly see the heart of God—self-giving love, inexhaustible mercy, unwavering faithfulness. Yet even as Christ reveals the Father, He continually draws us beyond what we have already understood. Every encounter with Him expands the heart, deepens wonder, and invites us to keep following.

Hold your convictions with gratitude, but hold them with open hands. The God who is Truth will never deceive you, yet He will always surprise you. His mystery is not a barrier keeping you away. It is the endless horizon calling you ever deeper into His love.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Living God*

> **Exhale:** *Lead me beyond my idols*
