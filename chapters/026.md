## Day 26 — Leave Some Mysteries to the Fire

> *Faith grows not by explaining everything, but by trusting the One who knows all things.*

The Passover meal contained an unusual command. The people were to eat what was given to them eagerly, yet they were not to break the bones. Whatever remained beyond their use was to be given back to the fire. Even in this meal, God was teaching the soul how to approach His mysteries.

Receive gladly what has been made clear. Love your neighbor. Forgive those who wound you. Pray without ceasing. Follow Christ wherever He leads. Feed deeply upon these truths until they become your strength for the journey. Do not delay obedience while waiting for every question to be answered.

Yet there are mysteries that no human mind can fully contain. You may wonder how God's infinite life has no beginning, why suffering lingers, or how eternity embraces time. These questions are not signs of weak faith. They remind you that the God you seek is infinitely greater than your understanding. The mountain always rises beyond the horizon because its summit cannot be exhausted.

Do not mistake mystery for absence. The hidden things belong not to darkness but to divine light too brilliant for your present sight. In time, what you need to know will be given. Until then, entrust the unopened depths to the Holy Spirit, who searches even the depths of God, and continue your ascent with peaceful confidence.

The soul grows most beautifully not when it possesses every answer, but when it delights to keep walking with the One who is Himself the Answer.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *I trust Your wisdom.*

> **Exhale:** *Lead me deeper into mystery.*
