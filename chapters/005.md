## Day 5 — Bread for Today

> *The soul is nourished one day at a time.*

After the sea had been crossed and the songs of victory had faded, the wilderness began to reveal its deeper purpose. There, where no fields could be planted and no harvest could be gathered, the people discovered that life depends upon more than what human hands can produce. Water flowed from the rock, and bread descended from heaven. God was teaching them that He Himself would become their daily provision.

You also will come to places where your own strength is no longer enough. At first this may seem like loss, but do not be discouraged. The wilderness is not meant to impoverish your soul but to free it from the illusion that it lives by its own resources. Every true gift comes from above. Christ, the true Bread from heaven, does not simply strengthen you for the journey—He gives Himself as the life of the journey.

Notice, too, the wisdom hidden in the manna. No one could store tomorrow's grace. Those who anxiously gathered more than they needed found that it spoiled in their hands. Learn from this. Much of your anxiety comes from trying to live tomorrow before it arrives. The Father asks you only to receive today's mercy, today's strength, and today's bread. His generosity is renewed each morning because He desires that your trust be renewed each morning as well.

Even the Sabbath became part of this lesson. On the day before their rest, God quietly supplied all they would need. Rest, then, is not neglecting your responsibilities but trusting that God is faithful even when your hands are still. There comes a time when striving must give way to confidence, and labor must yield to worship.

When the Amalekites came against Israel, Moses did not win the battle by strength of arms alone. As Joshua fought below, Moses lifted his hands above. When his hands grew weary, others held them up until victory came. Never imagine that prayer is separate from the work of life. The deepest victories are often won in the quiet lifting of the heart toward God, supported by the love and prayers of others.

Receive today what the Father places before you. Do not grasp for tomorrow. Christ is enough for this day, and when tomorrow comes, you will find that He is already there waiting for you.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Give me this day's bread.*

> **Exhale:** *You are enough.*
