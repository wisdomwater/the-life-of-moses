## Day 37 — The Mountain Calls Those Who Keep Climbing

> *Every victory of grace prepares you for a deeper vision of God.*

Do not imagine that the mountain stands at the beginning of the journey. Before Moses ascends into the cloud, he has already crossed the sea, tasted bitter waters made sweet, rested beneath the palms, drunk from the rock, eaten the bread from heaven, and stood firm in battle. So it is with you. God does not hurry you toward heights for which your heart is not yet prepared. Every trial, every act of trust, every quiet surrender becomes another step along the path that leads upward.

Perhaps you wonder why the vision of God sometimes seems distant. Do not mistake delay for rejection. The ascent itself is part of the gift. As you leave behind the false self and learn to live by Christ's life within you, your eyes slowly become capable of seeing what they once could not bear. The mountain has always been there, but now your heart is becoming ready to climb.

Do not think that reaching the mountain means you have arrived. The God who waits there is infinite. Every glimpse awakens a greater longing. Every answer opens into a deeper mystery. This is the joy of the soul's true life—not possessing God as though He could be contained, but endlessly receiving more of the One whose goodness has no boundary.

So keep walking with courage. Every faithful step through today's wilderness is already carrying you toward tomorrow's mountain. Christ does not simply call you upward; He ascends with you, transforming every step into a deeper participation in His own life.
