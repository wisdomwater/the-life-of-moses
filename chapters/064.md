## Day 64 — Return to the Rock

> *Every return to God begins where grace first met you.*

The wilderness has a way of revealing what still lives within us. Even after tasting the goodness of God, we sometimes find our hearts wandering back toward old comforts and familiar desires. We remember Egypt more fondly than we should, forgetting the chains that came with its pleasures. Yet the Lord does not abandon us when we lose our way. He patiently calls us to return.

Do not think that repentance means beginning your journey all over again. Repentance is returning to the Rock you once left behind. The living water has not dried up because your love has grown cold. Christ still stands ready to satisfy the thirst of every soul that turns again toward Him. The spring of grace has always been deeper than your failure.

Notice that Moses never stopped looking toward the promised inheritance. His hope remained fixed on the One foreshadowed by Joshua's cluster of grapes, the fruit that hung upon the wood for the life of the world. Because his eyes remained on that promise, he could lead others back to the water they had abandoned. Hope is always born from keeping our gaze upon Christ rather than upon ourselves.

So if you discover today that your heart has wandered, do not surrender to despair. Simply return. The Rock has not moved. The water still flows. Every act of repentance is not a retreat but another step toward the God whose mercy is always waiting to meet you.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Lead me back to the Rock.*

> **Exhale:** *Let me drink again.*
