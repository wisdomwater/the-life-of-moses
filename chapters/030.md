## Day 30 — When Bitter Waters Become Sweet

> *The cross does not remove every hardship; it transforms it.*

Do not lose heart when the first steps of following Christ seem harder than the life you left behind. Israel had escaped Egypt, yet the wilderness did not immediately greet them with abundance. Instead, they found water they could not drink. Freedom itself tasted bitter.

You may discover the same within your own soul. The old pleasures lose their hold, yet the deeper joys of God's kingdom have not fully blossomed. Prayer feels dry. Obedience feels costly. The wilderness seems to ask more than it gives. Do not mistake this season for God's absence. It is simply the place where your heart is learning a new taste.

Then the wood is cast into the water. The cross enters what was bitter, and suddenly the same water becomes sweet. Christ does not always remove the wilderness, but He fills it with hope. When His resurrection becomes the horizon of your life, sacrifice is no longer empty, patience is no longer wasted, and suffering is no longer meaningless. Even the hardest road begins to carry the quiet sweetness of His presence.

So do not flee the bitter waters too quickly. Invite the cross into them. You may discover that the place you feared would exhaust your soul becomes the very place where Christ teaches you to delight in Him above every passing pleasure.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Sweeten my heart, O Christ.*

> **Exhale:** *Teach me the joy of Your cross.*
