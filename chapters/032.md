## Day 32 — The Bread That Becomes What You Need

> *Christ never ceases to nourish the soul that hungers for Him.*

Notice when the manna appears. It does not fall while Egypt's provisions still remain. Only after the travelers have exhausted the food they carried from slavery does heaven open its hand. So it is with the soul. As long as we still believe our satisfaction can be found in the old ways of living—in applause, possessions, control, or endless distraction—we have little appetite for the bread God longs to give us. But when those familiar provisions fail, we discover that our deepest hunger has been leading us toward Christ all along.

Do not fear the emptiness that sometimes comes upon you. There are seasons when what once sustained your ambitions or comforts no longer satisfies. This is not God's absence but His invitation. He gently loosens your grip on lesser nourishment so that you may receive the Bread who comes down from heaven, not grown by human effort, but given freely by the Father's love.

This Bread is unlike any earthly food. Christ gives Himself differently to each heart without ever becoming divided. To the weary, He is rest. To the fearful, He is courage. To the childlike, He is gentle milk. To the mature, He is strong food that deepens wisdom. The same Lord meets every soul where it truly is, yet always draws it one step closer to His own life.

Come to Him with your real hunger, not the one you think you ought to have. He already knows what your soul needs, and His goodness is never exhausted. The more deeply you receive Him, the more your hunger itself becomes holy—a longing that carries you ever onward into the infinite feast of God.
