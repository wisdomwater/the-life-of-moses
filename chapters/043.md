## Day 43 — Christ, the Living Tabernacle

> *The dwelling place of God is not a building but a Person, and in Him we are welcomed home.*

When Moses stood upon the mountain, he was shown a sanctuary unlike anything human hands could build. Every pillar, every curtain, every lamp, and every sacred vessel reflected a deeper reality that could not be fashioned from wood, stone, or gold. The earthly tabernacle was beautiful, but its beauty came from what it pointed toward rather than from itself. Like a window catching the sunrise, it existed to reveal a greater light.

Do not stop at the symbols. Let them lead you to Christ. He is the true Tabernacle who existed before time yet willingly pitched His tent among us. The One who fills heaven accepted the limits of flesh so that those who had wandered far from life might be brought home again. God did not wait for humanity to climb the mountain; He descended into our wilderness and made His dwelling among us.

Perhaps you sometimes wonder where God truly lives. You search for Him in sacred places, profound experiences, or perfect understanding. Yet the mystery has already been revealed: the fullness of God has made its home in Christ. To draw near to Him is to enter the sanctuary for which every earthly temple was only a shadow.

Come, then, not merely to admire the tabernacle but to enter it. In Christ, the distance between heaven and earth has been bridged. Every step toward Him becomes a step into the living presence of God, where you are received not as a visitor but as one who belongs.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Christ, my dwelling place*

> **Exhale:** *Lead me into Your presence*
