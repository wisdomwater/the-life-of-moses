## Day 49 — Clothed in the Soul's True Beauty

> *The finest garments are woven from a life transformed by love.*

When Moses was shown the garments of the priesthood, he was not simply learning how a priest should appear. He was being shown how the soul is clothed when it draws near to God. Gold, precious stones, rich colors, and fine embroidery all speak of an inner beauty that no fabric can produce. The truest adornment is not worn upon the body but formed within the heart.

Do not spend your strength polishing what the world can see while neglecting the hidden person you are becoming. God delights in a beauty that cannot fade—a soul made gentle by mercy, steadfast through faith, humble in success, and patient in suffering. These virtues are the garments that never wear thin. They grow more radiant with every act of love.

The priest's robe stretched from head to foot without interruption, reminding us that holiness is not reserved for a single part of life. It is meant to cover everything. Let your prayers shape your work, your work shape your relationships, and your relationships reflect the love of Christ. The life of God is not something you put on for sacred moments alone; it is a garment woven through every ordinary day.

As you continue climbing the mountain, do not ask merely how you should behave. Ask who you are becoming. Christ does not simply improve the old self. He clothes you with His own life until, little by little, His beauty becomes visible through yours.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Clothe me in Christ*

> **Exhale:** *Make my heart beautiful*
