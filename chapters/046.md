## Day 46 — Living Stones in the House of Christ

> *Christ builds His dwelling not from stone, but from transformed lives.*

When Moses looked upon the heavenly tabernacle, he saw a pattern that would later be reflected on earth. Yet the deepest fulfillment of that vision is not found in a tent, a temple, or even a beautiful sanctuary. The true dwelling place of God is His people, gathered together in Christ. Each life surrendered to His love becomes part of a living house where His presence is made known.

Do not think that only a few remarkable saints are the pillars of this house. The apostles were indeed its first visible supports, but every soul that stands firm in faith strengthens the whole body. Whenever you forgive instead of retaliating, encourage instead of discouraging, or remain faithful through suffering, you become a quiet pillar that helps others remain standing. Light spreads from one faithful life to another.

This is how Christ continues to build His Church. He shapes ordinary people into places of refuge, hope, and stability. The strongest walls are not built with stone but with lives rooted in love. Perhaps you feel small or unnoticed, but every act of faithfulness adds another living stone to the house God is raising.

Do not ask whether you are important enough to serve Him. Ask only whether you are willing to let His grace make you steadfast. A single life filled with the light of Christ can illumine far more than it ever imagines.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Build me, Lord*

> **Exhale:** *Into Your living house*
