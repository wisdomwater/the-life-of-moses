## Day 22 — The Hands Stretched Wide

> *The cross reaches farther than our deepest captivity.*

The frogs that covered Egypt were more than a plague. They reveal how unchecked passions multiply until they seem to fill every corner of life. We know this experience. A single resentment becomes bitterness. A small indulgence grows into a habit. Before long, what once disgusted us begins to feel ordinary.

Yet notice what brings an end to the plague. Moses stretches out his hands, and death comes to what once enslaved the people. Lift your eyes beyond Moses, and you will see another with outstretched hands—the true Lawgiver, Christ upon the cross. His open arms do not merely reveal God's love; they become the place where our deepest chains begin to lose their strength.

When you turn your gaze toward Him, old passions begin to die. What once ruled you no longer speaks with the same authority. You may even look back on former ways of living with a quiet sorrow, wondering how you ever mistook them for life. This holy grief is not meant to shame you but to remind you how far grace has already carried you.

Do not linger over the stench of what has passed away. Leave it behind and continue walking. Christ has not freed you so that you might keep revisiting Egypt, but so that you might learn the freedom of His kingdom.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Lift my eyes to You.*

> **Exhale:** *Set my heart free.*
