## Day 58 — The Desire That Grows

> *To see God is to discover that there is always more of Him to love.*

You may think that desire should fade once it has been fulfilled. Earthly hunger works this way: we eat and are satisfied, we rest and are refreshed, we reach a destination and stop walking. But the desire for God follows another law. The more deeply you taste His goodness, the more your heart expands to receive what lies beyond.

This is the true vision of God: not possessing Him as something fully understood, but continually awakening to His infinite beauty. What you see today becomes the beginning of tomorrow’s longing. Every glimpse rekindles desire, and every grace enlarges the soul for a greater grace. God does not frustrate your hunger; He makes it capable of endless joy.

Do not be troubled when prayer leaves you longing for more. Do not assume that your thirst means God has withheld Himself. The Infinite cannot be contained by a single experience, a single insight, or even a lifetime of seeking. His goodness has no boundary, and therefore your growth in goodness need never end.

Let your longing become spacious. You do not need to force yourself toward some final spiritual achievement. Simply keep turning toward the beauty already given, and allow it to draw you onward. The desire itself is part of your communion with God.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Expand my heart.*

> Exhale: *Draw me into more.*
