## Day 70 — Blessed Beyond Every Curse

> *A life hidden in God cannot be defined by the voices that oppose it.*

Balaam came to pronounce a curse, yet blessing came from his lips instead. This is one of God's quiet triumphs. The powers that oppose His people cannot finally speak a truer word than the One who knows them completely. When a life is rooted in God, accusation loses its authority.

This does not mean that others will never misunderstand you or speak against you. Christ Himself was slandered, rejected, and condemned. But falsehood cannot change the reality of a heart being transformed by divine love. The truest testimony is not found in defending yourself but in quietly bearing the fruit of the Spirit.

Do not spend your strength chasing every criticism or answering every accusation. Instead, give your attention to becoming the kind of person against whom lies cannot endure. A life shaped by humility, mercy, patience, and truth becomes its own answer. Even those who oppose you may one day be compelled to acknowledge the grace they once resisted.

Rest, then, in the blessing that comes from Christ. You are not sustained by the opinions of others but by the faithful love of God. As you continue walking with Him, even what was meant to wound you may become another witness to His goodness. The blessing of God reaches farther than every curse and speaks more deeply than every accusing voice.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Your blessing is enough.*

> **Exhale:** *My life is hidden in You.*
