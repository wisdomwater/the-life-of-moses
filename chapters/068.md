## Day 68 — The Royal Way

> *The path of Christ is neither excess nor deficiency, but the steady road of love.*

As Moses led the people onward, the way narrowed. On either side lay dangerous cliffs, and safety was found not in wandering but in remaining on the road prepared for them. So it is with the spiritual life. We often imagine our greatest danger is moving backward, yet we may also lose our way by rushing too far ahead or by drifting into extremes. The narrow way is not restrictive because God delights in limits, but because love keeps us close to what gives life.

Do not think that virtue is found in intensity alone. One person is ruled by fear, another by reckless confidence, yet both have left the path. One mistakes harshness for holiness, another mistakes indulgence for freedom. Christ calls us to something better—a life so rooted in the Father that neither fear nor excess can pull us away from His peace.

This is why wisdom walks hand in hand with humility. Be as discerning as the serpent, yet as gentle as the dove. Hold conviction without becoming hard. Show mercy without abandoning truth. The life of Christ is not a compromise between opposites but their beautiful fulfillment, where strength is clothed in gentleness and truth is expressed through love.

The royal road is not found by constantly looking over the edge to see how close you can come to the cliffs. It is found by keeping your eyes on Christ, who walks before you. As your heart remains fixed on Him, your steps grow steady almost without your noticing. You discover that holiness is less about balancing yourself and more about following the One who never leaves the path.

Walk this road without anxiety. If you stumble, His hand is near. If the way seems narrow, remember where it leads. Every faithful step, however small, carries you farther into the endless country of God's life.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Keep me on Your path.*

> **Exhale:** *Lead me in Your love.*
