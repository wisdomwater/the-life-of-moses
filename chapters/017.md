## Day 17 — Help Comes to Meet You

> *When you turn toward freedom, grace is already walking toward you.*

At the beginning of his journey, Moses met conflict everywhere. He saw oppression, anger, and division, and even his attempts to help seemed to end in greater trouble. But after the wilderness had quieted him and the fire of God had illumined him, he returned to Egypt differently. This time, he did not meet another quarrel. His brother came peacefully to meet him.

There are seasons when the soul feels surrounded by opposing voices. One calls you toward patience, truth, and freedom; another promises the quick comfort of anger, appetite, or fear. The lesser voice often seems stronger because it offers something immediate and visible. Grace speaks more quietly, asking you to trust a goodness that has not yet fully appeared.

Yet you are not left alone between these voices. God has surrounded your life with hidden assistance. His Spirit reasons gently within you, Christ intercedes for you, and help often arrives through the presence of another person—a friend, a companion, a word spoken at the needed hour. This grace may have been near you all along, but you begin to recognize it when you turn your face toward the good.

Think of your soul as a mirror. When it is turned toward fear, it reflects fear. When it is turned toward the hope of God, His goodness begins to form its image within you. Virtue is not produced by lonely effort; it grows through participation, as the soul receives and reflects the life of Christ.

So do not be discouraged as you approach the places where old powers still resist your freedom. You will not face Pharaoh alone. Continue toward the work God has given you, and trust that somewhere along the road, brotherly grace is already coming to meet you.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Your grace comes near.*

> Exhale: *I do not walk alone.*
