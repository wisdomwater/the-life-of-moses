## Day 45 — The Light That Raises the Soul

> *Heaven's worship is already reaching toward you, lifting you into the life of God.*

The sanctuary Moses saw was alive with light. Lamps burned continually, heavenly beings surrounded the presence of God, and every part served the harmony of divine worship. This is no lifeless picture of heaven but a living reality. All creation is upheld by the love of God, and the unseen hosts rejoice to help those who are being drawn toward Him.

Do not imagine that you climb the mountain alone. Grace surrounds you in ways your eyes cannot yet perceive. Just as the sacred ark was carried by its poles, so your own soul is gently lifted by the unseen work of God. Many times you think you are holding on to Him, when in truth He has been carrying you all along.

The lamps of the sanctuary remind us that the Spirit shines with many rays yet from one source. Wisdom, understanding, counsel, strength, knowledge, reverence, and holy delight all flow from the same divine light. Christ does not merely illuminate your path from a distance; He kindles His own life within you until your heart begins to shine with His likeness.

And the worship of heaven never ceases. Prayer is not simply your words rising upward; it is your life joining a song that has always been sung. Every act of love, every quiet surrender, every whispered prayer becomes part of that eternal praise whose fragrance fills the true sanctuary. Even now, while your feet still walk the earth, your soul is learning the music of heaven.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Fill me with Your light*

> **Exhale:** *Lift me into Your praise*
