## Day 42 — The Tabernacle Not Made with Hands

> *Every ascent opens the way to a greater one.*

Moses does not stop when he reaches the mountain. He does not remain satisfied with the trumpet, the cloud, or even the holy darkness. He is led still farther, into the tabernacle not made with hands. See how the soul's journey unfolds. Every place that feels like an arrival quietly becomes the doorway to a deeper communion with God.

Do not fear that you have not yet reached the end. There is no disappointment in this endless ascent, because the One toward whom you are climbing is infinite. If God could be fully possessed, your joy would one day come to an end. But because His goodness has no limit, every new discovery awakens another. Eternity itself is the unending delight of growing ever more deeply into the life of God.

Even now, all creation joins this invitation. The heavens proclaim His glory without speaking a word. The beauty of the world is like a trumpet calling the heart to lift its eyes beyond the gift to the Giver. Every sunrise, every star, every act of love whispers that there is always more of God to receive than you have yet imagined.

So keep climbing with quiet confidence. Do not cling to yesterday's experiences, however precious they were. Christ is always leading you onward—from glory to glory, from faith to deeper faith, from love into ever greater love. The journey itself is becoming your home, for every step is taken with Him.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Lead me onward*

> **Exhale:** *From glory to glory*
