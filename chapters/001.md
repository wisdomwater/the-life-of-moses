## Day 1 — The Journey Without End

> *The soul grows most when it discovers there is no end to God's goodness.*

When we imagine perfection, we often picture a finish line. We imagine that one day we will finally become patient enough, loving enough, wise enough, and faithful enough to arrive. Yet the gospel paints a different picture. The closer we draw to God, the more we discover that His beauty stretches beyond every horizon we have yet seen. What once looked like an ending becomes another beginning.

This is not discouraging news—it is wonderful news. If God could be fully grasped, our joy would eventually come to an end. But because He is infinite love, every step toward Him awakens a deeper capacity to receive Him. Like Paul, we keep pressing forward, not because God remains distant, but because His goodness is endlessly abundant. Desire itself becomes part of the gift.

This is why spiritual growth is less about achieving perfection than about refusing to stop growing. Every act of forgiveness opens the heart a little wider. Every quiet prayer deepens our hunger. Every surrender loosens another chain of the false self and makes room for the life of Christ within us. The path is not measured by how far we have traveled but by whether we continue walking.

Moses will become our companion on this journey, but before we ever climb Sinai or enter the wilderness, Gregory invites us to set aside one illusion: that holiness is something we can complete. The image of God within us is always being renewed, always being enlarged, always being drawn further into the mystery of divine love. The destination is not a place where growth ends, but a life where love never ceases to deepen.

Today, take one peaceful step toward Christ. You do not need to see the entire mountain. It is enough to keep climbing, trusting that the God who calls you onward is also the One who walks beside you.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Lead me ever onward.*

> **Exhale:** *Into Your endless love.*
