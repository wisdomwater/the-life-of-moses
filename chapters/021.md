## Day 21 — The Heart That Chooses

> *God never forces the heart. Love always leaves room for freedom.*

You may wonder why some hearts seem to soften in the presence of God while others become even more resistant. It is tempting to imagine that God chooses tenderness for some and hardness for others. Yet this is not the way of divine love. Love never compels what only freedom can offer. The same sun that warms wax also hardens clay—not because the sun has changed, but because each receives its light according to its own nature.

Look at Pharaoh, not merely as a ruler from long ago, but as a mirror that sometimes reflects your own heart. Whenever pride refuses to surrender, whenever fear clings to control, the soul slowly becomes less able to hear the gentle voice that calls it home. This hardening is not something God inflicts from without. It is what happens when we repeatedly close the door to the One who patiently knocks.

Christ comes not to overpower your will but to awaken it. He stands before every heart with the same invitation, never coercing, always calling. Even now, every small act of trust opens another window to His light. The journey toward God is not the loss of your freedom but its fulfillment.

So do not fear that God is waiting to close His hand against you. Rather, ask whether there is any place within you that has quietly closed itself against Him. The door only needs to open a little for His mercy to begin its work.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Soften my heart.*

> **Exhale:** *I open to Your love.*
