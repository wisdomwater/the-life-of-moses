## Day 3 — Remove Your Sandals

> *God is most often found where reverence meets surrender.*

When Moses came upon the burning bush, he expected to witness a strange wonder. Instead, he encountered the living God. The fire blazed without consuming the branches, revealing a mystery that words can scarcely contain. Do not think that God's presence comes to destroy what He loves. His fire purifies, illumines, and fills with life. In Christ, the fire of divine love enters our humanity not to consume us, but to transform us until we shine with His own likeness.

Before Moses could draw nearer, he was told to remove his sandals. Learn from this, for no one ascends the mountain while clinging to everything that keeps the heart insulated from God. Pride, certainty, self-sufficiency, old resentments, and the identities we build for ourselves become like sandals upon our feet. Leave behind whatever prevents you from standing honestly before the Holy One.

Do not wait for extraordinary moments before expecting God to speak. Moses discovered the burning bush while faithfully tending sheep in an ordinary wilderness. The world is full of holy ground, but hurried hearts seldom notice it. Slow your pace, attend to the present moment, and allow your eyes to become receptive to grace.

As you continue your ascent, remember that you are not climbing toward a distant God but awakening to the One who has always been near. Every act of surrender opens the heart a little wider. Every step into humility prepares you for a deeper encounter with the infinite love revealed in Christ.

Stand quietly before the burning bush today. Remove whatever keeps your heart guarded, and let the fire of God's love make you more fully alive.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Here I am, Lord.*

> **Exhale:** *Make this heart holy.*
