## Day 9 — The Serpent Lifted Up

> *God can transform even the wounds of our wandering into the place where healing begins.*

Do not be surprised if the journey through the wilderness reveals what still lives within your heart. Again and again, the people forgot the goodness they had already received. Though heaven fed them and the rock gave them drink, they longed for Egypt's comforts and complained when the path became difficult. Such is the frailty of the human soul. We easily forget yesterday's grace when today's hunger demands our attention.

Yet Moses did not cease to pray for the people. He answered envy with gentleness, rebellion with patience, and accusation with intercession. Learn from him that true spiritual maturity is not measured by how little you are wounded by others, but by how deeply you continue to love them. This is the way that Christ Himself would one day walk, praying even for those who rejected Him.

When poisonous serpents entered the camp, the people experienced outwardly what had long been growing within them. Sin always promises satisfaction but leaves its venom behind. Still, God did not abandon them to the poison. He instructed Moses to lift up a bronze serpent so that whoever looked upon it in faith would live. Long afterward, our Lord revealed the mystery hidden within that sign: just as the serpent was lifted up in the wilderness, so the Son of Man would be lifted up upon the cross. There, the very instrument of death became the place where death itself was overcome.

Never believe that your failures place you beyond the reach of God's mercy. The Father does not heal by pretending your wounds do not exist. He invites you to look honestly upon them while fixing your gaze even more firmly upon Christ. As your eyes rest upon Him, the poison loses its power, shame gives way to hope, and what once brought death becomes the place where grace is most clearly revealed.

Therefore, when you become aware of your weakness, do not look away in despair. Lift your eyes to the Crucified One. The deeper your need, the greater your opportunity to discover the inexhaustible compassion of God, who never tires of leading His children from death into life.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *I lift my eyes to You.*

> **Exhale:** *Heal me with Your love.*
