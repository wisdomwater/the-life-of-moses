## Day 11 — Becoming Who You Choose to Be

> *Each day, your soul is being born again through the choices you make.*

You may think your life was determined long ago—by your family, your past, your wounds, or the circumstances into which you were born. Yet the story of Moses begins by showing us something deeper. His birth took place under the shadow of a tyrant who sought to destroy every promise before it could grow. So it is with every soul. There is always a voice that fears the life God longs to awaken within you, because every step toward freedom weakens the rule of fear.

Do not imagine that your true birth happened only once. You are continually becoming. Every choice you make shapes the person you are growing into. When you choose resentment, fear, or self-centeredness, you strengthen what keeps you in bondage. But whenever you choose love, humility, forgiveness, or trust, you allow a new self to be born—a life that reflects the image of Christ more clearly than before.

This birth is never effortless. Every genuine act of love feels like labor because it asks something of the old self. Yet do not mistake that struggle for failure. The pain is often the sign that something new is entering the world through you. The tyrant resists because he knows that every victory of grace loosens his hold.

So do not ask only, "Who have I been?" Ask instead, "Who am I becoming?" The Father has placed within you the freedom to answer that question anew today. Each faithful choice is another birth into His light, and every birth awakens a greater longing for the One in whose image you were created.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Form Your life in me.*

> **Exhale:** *Make me new today.*
