## Day 59 — Standing on the Rock

> *The soul rises most freely when it is firmly rooted in Christ.*

God tells Moses to stand upon the rock, yet this standing is also an ascent. At first this seems impossible. How can someone remain still and continue moving upward? But the spiritual life often advances through a steadiness that looks, from the outside, like stillness.

When your heart is uncertain, every passing fear can move you. You step forward and slide backward, like someone climbing a hill of loose sand. Much effort is spent, but little ground is gained. The soul needs something firmer than mood, opinion, success, or spiritual excitement.

Christ is the rock beneath your feet. To stand in Him is not to stop growing, but to grow from a place that cannot be washed away. The more deeply you remain in His mercy, the freer you become to forgive. The more securely you rest in His love, the more courageously you can surrender. Stability in the good becomes a kind of wing.

There will be seasons when progress does not feel dramatic. You may simply continue praying, loving, forgiving, and choosing what is true. Do not despise this quiet faithfulness. The heart rooted in Christ is already ascending, even when it seems only to be standing still.

**Breath Prayer**

> Inhale: *Plant me upon Christ.*

> Exhale: *Lift me through faithfulness.*
