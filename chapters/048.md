## Day 48 — Beyond the Veil

> *Faith does not remove mystery; it teaches us to dwell within it.*

At the center of the tabernacle was the Holy of Holies, a place hidden from ordinary sight. This sacred room reminds us that there is always something about God that remains beyond our grasp. The greatest realities cannot be captured by careful definitions or possessed by the mind. They are received through reverent love.

Do not be troubled when your understanding reaches its limits. The soul does not mature by explaining every mystery but by trusting the One who exceeds every explanation. There is wisdom in knowing where thought must become silence and where certainty must become worship.

As you continue your ascent with Moses, you will discover that every glimpse of God awakens an even greater longing. What once seemed like the end of the journey becomes another beginning. This is not because God is hiding from you, but because His goodness is infinite. Every step closer reveals new depths still waiting to be explored.

So stand before the veil without impatience. Believe that beyond all you can presently see is a love more real than your understanding can contain. The hidden sanctuary is not empty. Christ has entered it for you, and one day every shadow will give way to the joy of His unveiled presence.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Beyond what I know*

> **Exhale:** *I trust Your mystery*
