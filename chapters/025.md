## Day 25 — Travel Light

> *Those who have tasted the Lamb no longer make Egypt their home.*

Once the people had eaten the Passover lamb, they were not told to settle in for the evening. They were told to be ready to leave. Their sandals remained on their feet, their belts fastened around their waists, and their staffs held firmly in their hands. The meal was not the end of the journey but the beginning of it.

So it is with all who come to Christ. We are nourished by the true Lamb, not so that we may become comfortable where we are, but so that we may continue the journey toward the kingdom. The Christian life is never a permanent residence in this passing world. Every day carries us one step farther from Egypt and one step nearer to the endless life of God.

Wear the sandals of self-control, for the road is scattered with the sharp thorns of temptation. Fasten the belt of wisdom around your heart, lest the endless distractions of this age entangle your steps. Carry the staff of hope in your hand, for there will be long stretches of wilderness where hope alone will steady your feet. These are not burdens but the simple provisions of every pilgrim who walks with God.

Do not become so occupied with the comforts of the journey that you forget its destination. The things of this life are good when received with gratitude, but they are poor masters when they become our home. Hold them lightly. The freer your hands become, the more readily they can receive what God desires to give.

Walk on, then, with quiet joy. The One who feeds you is also the One who leads you. Every step away from Egypt is another step into the freedom for which you were created.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Strengthen me for the journey.*

> **Exhale:** *Lead me toward Your kingdom.*
