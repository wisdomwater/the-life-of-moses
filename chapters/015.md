## Day 15 — Learning What Truly Is

> *Everything that passes points beyond itself to the One who never passes away.*

You spend much of your life surrounded by things that promise permanence. Success, possessions, reputation, even your own strength seem solid for a time. Yet all created things are like shadows that move with the changing light. Beautiful though they are, they cannot bear the weight of your deepest hope because they do not possess life in themselves.

When Moses stood before the burning bush, he encountered the One who simply is. God alone is unchanging love, inexhaustible goodness, and everlasting life. Everything else exists because it shares in His being, just as the moon shines only because it reflects the sun. The more deeply the soul sees this, the less it clings to what cannot last and the more freely it rests in the One who never fades.

This is why the spiritual journey is not an escape from creation but a new way of seeing it. Every gift becomes more beautiful when it is received as a gift instead of mistaken for the Giver. Christ opens our eyes to behold the eternal shining through the passing moments of ordinary life.

Do not be discouraged when the things you once trusted prove unable to satisfy your heart. Their weakness is itself a gracious invitation. They are quietly pointing you toward the One for whom your soul has always been searching.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *You alone endure.*

> **Exhale:** *Anchor me in You.*
