## Day 62 — Trust the Better Report

> *Faith learns to listen to the voice that awakens hope rather than the voice that feeds fear.*

There are always two reports speaking within you. One remembers Egypt with surprising affection, recalling only the meals while forgetting the chains. The other speaks of a country not yet seen—a life of deeper freedom, fuller love, and greater communion with God. Do not be surprised that these voices compete for your attention. The enemy cannot always turn your feet backward, so he seeks instead to turn your heart toward what once enslaved you.

See how the people longed for the food of Egypt while the bread from heaven still lay before them. Such is the deception of the old self. It forgets the bitterness of bondage and remembers only the comforts that accompanied it. Every step toward God asks us to release another attachment that once seemed indispensable. What first feels like loss slowly becomes the discovery of a richer feast.

Moses lifted his eyes beyond the wilderness because his heart had already begun to dwell in the promised inheritance. So let your deepest thoughts become like faithful spies, searching not for reasons to despair but for signs of God's coming goodness. Feed your imagination with the promises of Christ more than the fears of your circumstances. Hope grows wherever faith is allowed to linger.

You will always find voices eager to tell you that transformation is impossible, that holiness is beyond your reach, or that your journey has already gone too far astray. Listen instead for the quiet testimony of the Spirit, who continually whispers that there is still more land to possess, more love to receive, and more of God to discover. The better report is not naïve optimism. It is faith seeing farther than fear can see.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Teach me to trust Your promise.*

> **Exhale:** *Lead me into Your inheritance.*
