## Day 47 — The Fragrance of a Holy Life

> *The sweetest offering to God is a heart being transformed by love.*

The worship of God's people is more than songs sung together or prayers spoken aloud. The incense that rises before Him is every heart lifted in gratitude, every quiet act of mercy, every hidden surrender known only to Him. Morning and evening, the life turned toward God becomes a continual offering whose fragrance fills His house.

The cleansing waters also continue to flow. Baptism is not merely a memory but the beginning of a life continually washed by grace. Again and again Christ meets us where we have become stained by fear, pride, or selfishness, inviting us back into the freshness of His life. Those who extend that mercy to others become, in a beautiful way, living fountains through whom God's cleansing love reaches the world.

The beauty of the tabernacle was completed by coverings dyed red and woven from coarse hair. They remind us that love often grows through surrender rather than comfort. As the old patterns of the false self lose their hold, the life of Christ becomes more visible within us. Self-control, humility, and quiet faithfulness may seem plain to the world, but in God's eyes they adorn His dwelling with uncommon beauty.

Do not despise the hidden work of transformation. Every passion surrendered, every temptation resisted, and every act of love freely given becomes another thread in the tapestry God is weaving from your life.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Purify my heart*

> **Exhale:** *Let my life be praise*
