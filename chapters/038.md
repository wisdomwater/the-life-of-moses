## Day 38 — Wash Your Garments

> *The clearest vision of God belongs to the purest heart.*

Before Moses ascends the mountain, he is told to wash his garments. Do not suppose that God is concerned with dust upon cloth. The garments are the outward life—the habits, words, relationships, and choices by which the hidden condition of the heart becomes visible. The soul that longs to behold God cannot be content with inward devotion while neglecting the life it lives before others.

Yet purity is far deeper than outward respectability. The heart itself must be washed of every false image it has gathered along the way. Pride, resentment, fear, and every distorted thought about God cloud the inner eye. Christ does not cleanse you merely to make you acceptable. He cleanses you so that you may truly see.

There comes a point on the journey when even the senses can carry you no farther. The beauty of creation, the sound of sacred songs, and the comfort of familiar practices are precious gifts, but they are not God Himself. At the mountain's heights, you are invited beyond what can be grasped by sight or hearing into the quiet wonder where faith learns to rest in the One who surpasses every image and every thought.

Do not be afraid when prayer grows simpler and words become fewer. This is often how the mountain begins. As the noise within you grows quiet, Christ gently opens your heart to a communion deeper than understanding—a knowing born not of explanation, but of love.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Wash my heart*

> **Exhale:** *That I may see You*
