## Day 29 — Leave Egypt Beneath the Waters

> *The waters of new life are meant to bury the old life, not carry it forward.*

When Israel entered the sea, they did not merely escape Pharaoh—they passed through a place where an old story came to its end. They emerged on the other shore alive, but the army that had enslaved them remained beneath the waters. This is the mystery hidden within our own journey with Christ. God does not simply help us manage our captivity. He leads us into a new life where our deepest chains no longer define who we are.

Do not think of Pharaoh's army as something far away in history. It marches within every heart. Pride gives its commands. Anger sharpens its weapons. Fear drives its horses. Greed demands more than it can ever possess. Every passion that promises freedom while making us less able to love belongs to that same army. Left unchecked, these masters will follow us even to the edge of the sea.

But Christ has opened a way through the waters. In Him, baptism is more than a sacred ceremony; it is the declaration that the old tyrants no longer have the final word. Each day you are invited to step more deeply into the reality of that gift. Whenever you forgive instead of retaliating, choose generosity over grasping, or answer fear with trust, another soldier of Pharaoh loses its hold upon your soul.

Do not be content merely to stand on the far shore while secretly inviting Egypt to travel with you. Many begin the journey of faith yet continue serving the same old masters under a different name. Freedom grows wherever you surrender another corner of your heart to Christ. The sea is crossed not once only, but again and again, as grace teaches you to leave yesterday's chains beneath the waters.

Walk forward, then, without looking back. The One who has delivered you is also transforming you. Every step away from slavery is another step into the likeness of the One who has called you His own.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Lead me through the waters.*

> **Exhale:** *Leave my old life behind.*
