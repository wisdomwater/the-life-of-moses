## Day 2 — Choosing Your True Home

> *The first step toward freedom is remembering who you truly belong to.*

Every soul begins its journey surrounded by powers that seek to shape it into something less than the image of God. Moses was born under a ruler who feared life itself, yet the hand of God quietly guarded him before he could even take his first steps. Do not think that your story began only when you first became aware of God. Long before you sought Him, His providence was already seeking you, carrying you through dangers you could neither see nor understand.

I, too, learned from Moses that we may spend years surrounded by the wisdom and splendor of Egypt without belonging to it. Moses received the finest education Pharaoh could offer, yet when the moment came, he remembered his true family. So it is with you. The world will gladly define you by your success, your failures, your possessions, or the opinions of others. But your deepest identity is found in the Father who fashioned you in His own image and calls you His beloved.

This remembrance will sometimes place you at odds with the kingdoms around you. Moses chose to stand beside the oppressed rather than enjoy the comforts of power. He sought peace among his own people, yet his efforts were misunderstood and rejected. Do not lose heart when faithfulness is met with misunderstanding. Such moments often become the doorway into a deeper work of God.

Notice where Moses next finds himself—not upon a throne, but in the wilderness. There, far from the noise of ambition and the praise of crowds, his soul becomes quiet enough to see what could not be seen in Egypt. Do not despise your own wilderness. It is often there that Christ gently loosens the grip of the false self so that your true life may emerge.

If you find yourself in such a place today, remain there with hope. The silence may be preparing your eyes to recognize the fire that has been burning before you all along.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Teach me who I am.*

> **Exhale:** *I belong to You.*
