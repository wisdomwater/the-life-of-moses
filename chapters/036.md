## Day 35 — Lift Your Eyes to the Cross

> *Victory belongs to the heart that keeps its gaze upon Christ.*

Israel prevailed as long as Moses' hands remained lifted toward heaven. When they fell, the people faltered. Do not imagine that the strength of the battle lay in Moses' arms. The victory came because the people's eyes were continually drawn upward, away from themselves and toward the God who fought with them.

The same temptation faces every soul. It is easy to let your vision sink until faith becomes nothing more than outward habit, rule-keeping, or anxious effort. When your eyes remain fixed only upon the letter, your heart grows weary beneath its weight. But when the Spirit lifts your understanding, the same Scriptures become alive with Christ. What once seemed a burden becomes an invitation into love.

Look closely at Moses standing with outstretched arms. Even before the cross was raised upon Calvary, its shape was already written into the story. The Law itself stretched out its hands toward Christ, revealing that its deepest purpose was never mere obedience to commands, but participation in redeeming love. Every page points beyond itself to the One whose open arms embrace the world.

When your own hands grow tired, do not lower your gaze. Lift your eyes again to the Crucified One. There you will find not only forgiveness for yesterday, but strength for today's battle and hope for tomorrow's journey. The cross is not merely the beginning of your salvation; it remains the banner beneath which you learn to live.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *I lift my eyes*

> **Exhale:** *To Christ my victory*
