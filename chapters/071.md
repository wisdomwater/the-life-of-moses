## Day 71 — Perfection Without End

> *The soul becomes perfect not by reaching the end, but by never ceasing to grow in love.*

You may wonder when the journey with God will finally be complete. Surely there must come a day when the climbing ends and there is nothing left to learn. Yet this is not the perfection to which Christ calls you. Because God is infinite, every step closer to Him opens another horizon of beauty. The soul does not become weary of this endless ascent; it becomes more alive with every step.

Do not think of perfection as flawless performance or spiritual arrival. Such a goal would leave either pride for those who imagine they have attained it or despair for those who know they have not. True perfection is something far more joyful. It is a heart so captivated by God's goodness that it never loses its desire to move deeper into His life.

Look at Moses. Every encounter with God prepared him for another. Every mountain revealed a higher summit. Every revelation awakened a greater mystery. Like an eagle rising above the clouds, he discovered that there was always more sky than he had yet flown through. So it is with every disciple of Christ. Growth does not end because love has no boundary.

Do not be discouraged that you still have far to go. Your longing itself is a sign that God is drawing you onward. The Spirit who has brought you this far will not cease His work until every corner of your life reflects the beauty of Christ—and even then, eternity itself will be an endless unfolding of His goodness.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Draw me ever nearer.*

> **Exhale:** *Your love has no end.*
