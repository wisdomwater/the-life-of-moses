## Day 27 — Carry Egypt's Gold into the Sanctuary

> *Whatever is true belongs to God, wherever it is found.*

Do not imagine that leaving Egypt means rejecting everything you found there. Bondage must be left behind, but truth need not be. When Israel departed, they carried Egypt's treasures with them—not to preserve Egypt's glory, but to dedicate those riches to the worship of the living God. In this, the Lord teaches the soul a wisdom that is easily forgotten.

As you journey with Christ, you will discover beauty, wisdom, and insight in unexpected places. A poem may awaken your heart. A scientist may uncover the wonder of creation. A philosopher may ask a question that leads you to deeper humility. A work of art may stir your longing for eternity. Do not fear these gifts simply because they come from beyond the borders of your own tradition. Every ray of truth has its source in the same eternal Light.

Yet do not mistake the treasure for the homeland. Gold taken from Egypt was never meant to build another palace for Pharaoh. It was meant to adorn the tabernacle where God would dwell among His people. In the same way, every truth you discover should become an offering that leads you more deeply into Christ. Learning that feeds pride becomes another chain. Learning that becomes worship is transformed into holy gold.

Walk through the world with open eyes and a discerning heart. Receive gratefully whatever is good, beautiful, and true, but carry it always toward the sanctuary. When every gift is laid before Christ, nothing worthy is lost. Instead, all things find their true purpose in the One through whom all wisdom was made.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Teach me what is true.*

> **Exhale:** *I offer it back to You.*
