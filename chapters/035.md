## Day 35 — Learning to Stand and Fight

> *The God who delivers you also teaches you to stand.*

There comes a time in every soul's journey when the battle changes. At first, you are too weak to fight. God delivers you while you can scarcely do anything but cry for help. He parts seas you cannot cross, sweetens bitter waters you cannot heal, and feeds you with bread you did not earn. Do not be ashamed that your first steps are carried by grace. Every child must first be held before he can walk.

But notice what follows. After freedom has begun to take root, after your heart has tasted the goodness of God, you are no longer called merely to watch another fight on your behalf. You are invited to stand. The enemies that once enslaved you—fear, resentment, pride, despair, and every false attachment—must now be met with the strength that God Himself has formed within you. This is not self-reliance. It is grace becoming courage.

See also who leads the battle. Moses, the faithful servant, prepared the way, but Joshua becomes the commander. So too the Law can awaken us, instruct us, and lead us out of bondage, but it cannot bring us into the fullness of victory. Christ, whose very name Joshua foreshadowed, is the One who leads us into the true inheritance. He does not merely tell us what righteousness looks like; He shares His own life with us so that we may become righteous from within.

Do not be discouraged when you discover there are still battles after your deliverance. The struggle itself is evidence that you are no longer a slave. Walk beside Christ, your true Captain. Each victory over the false self is another step into the freedom for which you were created.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Lead me, Lord*

> **Exhale:** *I will follow*
