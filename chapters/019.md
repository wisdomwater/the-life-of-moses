## Day 19 — The Same River, Different Hearts

> *The light of God reveals the heart that receives it.*

As Moses confronted Pharaoh, the miracles were never displays of power for their own sake. They revealed what was already hidden within those who witnessed them. The same act that awakened hope in Israel hardened the resistance of Egypt. God's truth does not change from one person to another, but the heart that receives it determines whether it becomes light or remains darkness.

So it is whenever Christ draws near. His words are living water, yet they are not received in the same way by every soul. A heart that longs for Him finds refreshment, even in difficult seasons. A heart determined to cling to its own illusions may taste the very same water and find only bitterness. The difference does not lie in the river but in the vessel brought to it.

Do not be surprised, then, when truth is misunderstood or even distorted. There have always been voices that mix the clear water of the Gospel with fear, pride, or falsehood. Yet no deception can extinguish the life that flows from Christ. Those who continue to seek Him with humility discover that beneath every muddy current, the living water remains pure.

Rather than asking whether God is speaking, ask whether your heart is becoming quiet enough to hear Him. The soul that desires truth more than being right will find itself continually cleansed by the river of His grace.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Open my heart.*

> **Exhale:** *Let me receive Your truth.*
