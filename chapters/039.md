## Day 39 — Hearing the Trumpet

> *The nearer you draw to God, the more clearly His voice is heard.*

As Moses climbs, the trumpet grows louder. This may seem strange, for we often imagine that God's voice fades into silence as we enter the mystery. Yet the opposite is true. The closer you come, the more clearly you hear the call of divine love. What once sounded faint becomes unmistakable. The heart, purified by grace, discovers that God has been speaking all along.

The first notes of that trumpet echoed through the Law and the Prophets, awakening hope for what was still to come. Then Christ appeared, and the melody reached its fullness. In Him, every promise found its living voice. The apostles carried that sound into the world, and it has never ceased to echo wherever hearts are open to receive it.

Not everyone is ready to climb the same height at the same time. There is wisdom in learning from those who have walked farther before you. Just as Israel listened to Moses, so the Church receives the witness of saints who have learned, through humility and prayer, to hear the voice of God more clearly. Their lives encourage us, not to remain where we are, but to continue the ascent ourselves.

Guard your heart against the temptation to grasp mysteries before you are willing to be transformed by them. Knowledge without humility soon becomes a burden, but a heart that patiently walks with Christ will discover that every step of obedience opens the ears a little wider. Keep climbing. The trumpet of God's love grows clearer with every faithful step.
