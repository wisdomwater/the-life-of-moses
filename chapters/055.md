## Day 55 — Holy Dissatisfaction

> *The closer you come to God, the deeper your longing becomes.*

You may wonder why your desire for God has not faded after so many years. You have known moments of His presence. You have seen prayers answered. You have climbed mountains you once thought impossible. Yet something within you still whispers, "There is more." Do not mistake this longing for failure. It is the mark of a soul awakening to the Infinite.

Look at Moses. He spoke with God as a friend speaks with a friend, yet he still prayed, "Show me Your glory." He did not ask because God had been absent, but because every encounter with God enlarged his capacity to desire Him still more. Divine love never leaves the soul satisfied in the way earthly pleasures do. Instead, every taste awakens a greater hunger.

This is the strange joy of the spiritual life. We are not climbing toward a finish line where desire finally ends. We are being drawn into a love without limits. Every grace becomes the doorway to another grace. Every glimpse of Christ stirs the heart to seek Him more deeply. The journey itself becomes part of the gift.

Do not be discouraged if your heart still reaches beyond what it has already received. The longing you feel is not emptiness but invitation. It is the gentle pull of God, whose infinite beauty will forever call you onward into deeper joy.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** Draw me ever nearer.

> **Exhale:** My longing is Your gift.
