## Day 7 — Build What You Have Seen

> *What God reveals within you, He desires to shape through you.*

Do not think that Moses climbed the mountain only to receive commandments. After his heart had been lifted beyond visible things into the mystery of God, he was shown a sanctuary of breathtaking beauty. It was not fashioned by human hands, nor was it the invention of human imagination. Heaven itself became his pattern. Then he was told to descend and build on earth what he had first beheld in the presence of God.

This is the way of every soul that desires to follow Christ. Prayer is never an escape from the world. If you truly behold the beauty of God, you will return with new hands, a new heart, and new eyes. The life hidden within becomes visible without. Your words grow gentler, your judgments more merciful, your work more honest, and your love more patient. The sanctuary God builds is not first a place, but a person being renewed into His likeness.

Even the smallest details of the tabernacle mattered. Its colors, its furnishings, the garments of the priest—none were without purpose. Learn from this that God is present not only in life's great moments but also in its quiet faithfulness. The beauty of holiness is woven one thread at a time, often through ordinary acts that seem unnoticed by the world.

Above all, remember the law that gives life to every other commandment: love your neighbor. Every command finds its fulfillment there. The heart that truly loves has already begun to resemble the sanctuary where God delights to dwell. Whatever draws you away from love draws you away from the pattern shown on the mountain.

Therefore, do not be content merely to admire heavenly things. Carry them into your daily life. Let Christ fashion your heart into a living sanctuary where His presence is made visible to all who enter.

**Breath Prayer**

> **Inhale:** *Shape me by Your presence.*

> **Exhale:** *Make me Your dwelling.*
